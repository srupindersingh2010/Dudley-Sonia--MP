About this site & its sources

This is an independent public information prototype run by {{config.operator}}. It is not the MP’s official website. Sonia Kumar’s office approval has not been confirmed.

It brings together public records for Dudley residents, inspired by the Lower Stoke Ward Newsletter. Automatic sections are factual and neutral. No party campaigning content or House of Commons portcullis logo is used.

View the code, data and source audit on GitHub. Report factual or accessibility problems through the repository’s Issues page; do not post personal casework there.

How updates work

A GitHub Actions job is scheduled daily at 07:00 Europe/London, including British Summer Time. GitHub may start scheduled runs late. Sources publish at their own pace: a daily retrieval does not mean the underlying statistic is new.

Each source is fetched separately. If retrieval fails, the last successful JSON snapshot is retained with its original retrieval date. If no successful snapshot exists, the section links to the original source and states that the feed is unavailable.

The website uses the most recent successful build. Source failures are listed below; a site build or deployment failure leaves the previously deployed site online.

Coverage & interpretation

  • Public records can contain errors, corrections and reporting delays. The linked original is authoritative.
  • Parliament’s roles data may not include all PPS appointments or APPG officer positions. No unverified role is claimed.
  • Voting participation measures recorded votes, not physical presence, effort or effectiveness.
  • Petitions are ranked by signatures attributed to Dudley’s current constituency code E14001204. Signatures are not an opinion poll.
  • Council and WMCA calendars cover wider administrative areas. They are not the MP’s diary.
  • Crime locations and Parliament’s constituency outline are approximate. Monthly reporting may lag several months.
  • Profile figures must use the current constituency geography and show their reference period; borough figures are not substituted.
  • News headlines and links identify outside reporting. They do not imply endorsement; publication dates are not event dates.

Reuse & accessibility

Parliamentary information is attributed to UK Parliament under the applicable Open Parliament Licence. Portrait use is subject to the Parliamentary Copyright Licence. Police data is supplied under the Open Government Licence. News publishers retain copyright in their material; we link to their originals.

Pages use semantic headings, keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators, readable text and high-contrast colours. Content works without JavaScript. Full WCAG 2.2 AA certification has not been independently audited.

What this site stores

The published JSON files contain public parliamentary records, public meeting information, linked news metadata and aggregate or anonymised public statistics. We do not invite you to submit constituent casework or other sensitive information.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, in accordance with its privacy statement. This site does not run a separate visitor tracking service.

Following an external link takes you to that provider’s website and privacy rules. Email and telephone links use your own device. Public social accounts are linked rather than embedded. The crime map is drawn locally from public data and loads no external map tiles.
